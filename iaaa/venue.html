Introduction to FPT University Danang Campus (FUDA)

FPT University's Da Nang campus is located in the FPT Urban Area, Ngu Hanh Son Ward, Da Nang City, with a 5.1-hectare site that began construction in 2018. The campus is equipped with modern amenities such as: an artificial turf football field (1,500 m², 5-7 players) with lighting, nets, rest areas, and parking; a basketball court, two volleyball courts, and a street workout area for sports activities; and a modern library with a spacious, quiet study environment and over 33,000 books, international textbooks, ebooks, and specialized materials. The dynamic and convenient learning environment helps students develop comprehensively in both knowledge and physical well-being.

IAAA 2027

The Third International Conference on Intelligent Aerial Access and Applications (IAAA) will be held in the dynamic and culturally vibrant city of Danang, Vietnam, from July 10-12, 2027, to gather scholars who address the current state of technology and the outcome of ongoing research in the fields. IAAA 2027 is hosted by FPT University Danang Campus and co-organized by Vietnam National University of Agriculture (VNUA), Hanoi University of Mining and Geology (HUMG) and Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology (HCMUT). Accepted and presented papers will be submitted for possibly published by Springer in series Lecture Notes in Networks and Systems (LNNS), indexed by SCOPUS.
