Profile

Academic biography

Luong Vuong Nguyen is a Senior Lecturer and Researcher at the Faculty of Artificial Intelligence, FPT University, Da Nang, Viet Nam, and an Adjunct Professor in the Department of Artificial Intelligence at The Catholic University of Korea. He received his Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering from Chung-Ang University in 2022.

His research interests include recommender systems, knowledge graphs, machine learning, deep learning, ambient intelligence, data mining, and logical reasoning.
